Privacy policy

Last updated 4 August 2026

The short version. Pin Me has no account system, no server of its own, and no analytics or advertising code. The places you save, the notes you write and your location are stored on your phone and are never sent to us. We could not look at your data if we wanted to — there is nowhere for it to arrive.

The rest of this page explains that in detail, including the three places where your phone does talk to somebody else.

What Pin Me stores, and where

Everything the app knows about you lives in your device's own app storage:

  • Your saved places — the name, optional note, emoji, coordinates and reminder radius for each place.
  • Your reminder history — a local record of when a reminder fired, so the app can show it on a place and avoid notifying you repeatedly about the same spot.
  • Your settings — whether reminders are paused, and whether you have finished the first-run introduction.

This data is written to your device only. It is not backed up to a service we run, it is not synced between devices, and it is not transmitted anywhere. If your device backs itself up to iCloud or to Google, that backup is governed by Apple's or Google's terms, not ours.

How your location is used

Pin Me asks for location access twice, and for two different reasons.

While using the app

Needed to show where you are on the map and to work out how far away your saved places are. Your position is held in memory while the app is open and is not written to storage or transmitted.

In the background

This is the permission that makes the app worth having. When you grant it, Pin Me registers your saved places as geofences with your phone's operating system. The operating system — not Pin Me — watches for you crossing into one of those circles, and wakes the app just long enough to post a local notification.

This means the app is not following you around, and there is no continuous location trail being recorded. Pin Me only learns that you arrived somewhere you had already told it you cared about. Background location is entirely optional: decline it and everything else still works, but reminders will only fire while the app is open.

You can pause all reminders with a single switch in Settings, or revoke location access at any time in your phone's system settings.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, created on your device by your device. Pin Me does not use push notifications, does not register a push token, and does not ship the push entitlement at all. No notification you receive has passed through a server.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, email addresses or passwords.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or marketing SDKs.
  • No selling, sharing or disclosing of personal data — we hold none.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Where your phone does talk to others

In the interest of being straight with you, there are three points where using the app involves a third party. None of them receive your saved places or notes.

  • Map tiles. The map is drawn using your platform's own maps component — Apple Maps on iOS, Google Maps on Android. Displaying a map means that provider serves the imagery for the area you are looking at, under Apple's or Google's privacy policy.
  • Directions. If you tap “Directions” on a place, the app hands that place's coordinates to your maps app to route you there. That is a deliberate action you take, and it only happens when you tap it.
  • App updates. The app checks for over-the-air updates from Expo's update service (u.expo.dev). Like any network request this reveals your IP address and basic app and device version information to that service. It carries none of your places, notes or location.

Your rights and your data

Because your data never leaves your device, you already have complete control of it — there is no request to file and nobody to ask.

  • To see your data: it is all visible in the app.
  • To correct it: edit or move any place from its detail screen.
  • To delete some of it: delete an individual place, which also removes its reminder history.
  • To delete all of it: use “Delete all data” in Settings, or simply uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything Pin Me has stored.

If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. We can honour them only in the sense described above, because we are not in possession of any personal data relating to you. If you believe otherwise, please get in touch — and you always have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority (in Denmark, Datatilsynet).

Children

Pin Me is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this page does not describe, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
